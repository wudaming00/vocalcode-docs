## Listing copy

- **Name:** VocalCode
- **Tagline:** Push-to-talk voice input for coding with AI.
- **Website:** <https://vocalcode.app/>
- **Pricing:** Paid, with a free trial
- **Topics:** Developer Tools; Productivity; Artificial Intelligence

### Description

Hold a configured key or mouse button, speak, and release. VocalCode recognizes
speech on your machine after the selected model downloads, cleans the text, and
inserts it into the active field in most desktop apps. It is built for dictating
AI coding prompts, documentation, and other prose. Available for Windows 10/11
x64 and Apple silicon macOS 11+. Try every feature for 30 days, then pay $4.99
once with no subscription.

## Launch guardrails

- Do not claim accuracy, speed, or compatibility that has not been measured.
- Do not describe recognition as fully offline until after the selected model
  has downloaded; licensing and update checks still require internet access.
- Do not publish a launch date until the release build and support inbox are
  ready for the first-day traffic.
- Reply to feedback as the maker. Do not incentivize votes or reviews.
