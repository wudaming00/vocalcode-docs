### Description

VocalCode (also written Vocal Code) is push-to-talk voice input for coding with
AI on Windows and Apple silicon Mac. Hold a configured key or mouse button,
speak, and release; VocalCode inserts the recognized text into the active field
in most desktop apps.

Recognition runs on your machine after the selected model downloads. Try every
feature for 30 days, then pay $4.99 once with no subscription.

Official site: https://vocalcode.app/
Privacy: https://vocalcode.app/privacy/
Documentation: https://github.com/wudaming00/vocalcode-docs
