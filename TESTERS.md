# Native-language tester program

VocalCode ships a 25-language European model (Parakeet TDT v3) plus a dedicated bilingual Chinese-English model. I can personally verify Chinese and English; the other languages are marked "preview" until a native speaker has actually tested them. That is where you come in.

## The deal

- **You**: a native (or daily-fluent) speaker of any language below, on Windows 10/11 or Apple Silicon macOS.
- **The test** (~10 minutes): dictate 5 everyday sentences, 5 sentences mixing your language with English technical terms, and 1 minute of free speech. Then say honestly how it went.
- **You get**: a permanent license, free, regardless of your verdict. Negative results are just as useful as positive ones. Every completed test qualifies - no cap, no "first N testers".

## Languages

Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Czech, Romanian, Greek, Hungarian, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Slovak, Croatian, Bulgarian, Lithuanian, Latvian, Estonian, Maltese.

## How to take part

1. Download VocalCode from https://vocalcode.app/download/ (winget / Homebrew / Scoop / direct installer). The 30-day trial is the full app - no card, no account.
2. Pick your language in Settings; the model downloads once.
3. Run the three-part test above.
4. Email **support@vocalcode.app** with subject `Native test: [your language]` - include your sentences, what came out, and your honest verdict.

Full instructions also live at https://vocalcode.app/testers/.
